# SOURCES — Lists of Open Problems in Physics

A curated knowledge graph of **canonical lists, roadmaps, and prize problems** that communities have used to organize open questions in physics (and physics-adjacent mathematical physics).

Each entry records: **year**, **author/org**, **count** (if known), **URL**, **domain**, and **status notes**. Status is coarse: *solved / partially solved / still open / program (not a finite checklist)*.

## 1. Historical lists (pre-2000 foundations)

### 1.1 Lord Kelvin — “Two clouds” (1900)

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| **Year** | 1900 (lecture; published 1901) |
| **Author** | William Thomson (Lord Kelvin) |
| **Title** | *Nineteenth Century Clouds over the Dynamical Theory of Heat and Light* |
| **N** | 2 |
| **Domain** | Foundations of classical physics |
| **URL** | Discussion: https://pubs.aip.org/aapt/ajp/article-abstract/89/11/1037/859801 ; historical text widely reprinted |
| **Problems** | (1) Motion of Earth through luminiferous ether / elastic-solid ether; (2) Maxwell–Boltzmann equipartition (failures for specific heats / energy partition) |
| **Status** | **Solved (in spirit)**: cloud 1 → special relativity; cloud 2 → quantum mechanics (equipartition / specific heats). Note: popular retellings often misstate cloud 2 as black-body radiation specifically; Kelvin’s wording targets equipartition. |
| **Role in landscape** | Archetype of “almost-finished physics” that exploded into two revolutions. |

### 1.2 Hilbert’s sixth problem (1900)

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| **Year** | 1900 |
| **Author** | David Hilbert |
| **Title** | Problem 6 among 23 *Mathematische Probleme* (ICM Paris) |
| **N** | 1 program (with many sub-programs) |
| **Domain** | Axiomatization of physics / mathematical physics |
| **URL** | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert%27s_sixth_problem ; recent kinetic sub-result: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01800 |
| **Content** | Treat physics “by means of axioms” where mathematics is ready; historically includes kinetic theory → hydrodynamics, probability foundations, and later QFT/GR axiomatization programs |
| **Status** | **Open as a program**. Partial milestones: rigorous kinetic→fluid limits in restricted settings (e.g. Deng–Hani–Ma 2025 claim for Boltzmann→fluids on tori); probability axiomatized (Kolmogorov); full interacting QFT in 4D and quantum gravity remain open. Never a finite checklist. |

### 1.4 Penrose — Unsolved problems in classical GR (1982)

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| **Year** | 1982 |
| **Author** | Roger Penrose |
| **Title** | *Some Unsolved Problems in Classical General Relativity* (in *Seminar on Differential Geometry*, Ann. of Math. Stud. 102) |
| **N** | ~dozen core themes; Wikipedia: “about 14 remain unsolved as of 2017” |
| **Domain** | Mathematical / classical general relativity |
| **URL** | Bibliographic: Penrose 1982, Ann. of Math. Studies 102, pp. 631–668 |
| **Key themes** | Cosmic censorship (weak/strong), quasi-local mass/energy, black-hole uniqueness/stability, Cauchy problem / global existence, singularities, chronology |
| **Status** | **Mostly still open** as precise theorems. Major progress: nonlinear stability of Minkowski (Christodoulou–Klainerman), many positive-mass results, substantial progress on Kerr stability and weak censorship in restricted classes; strong cosmic censorship and full final-state conjecture remain active frontiers. |

### 1.5 Bartnik — Open problems in mathematical relativity (1988)

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| **Year** | 1988 (conference; proc. 1989) |
| **Author** | Robert Bartnik |
| **Title** | *Some open problems in mathematical relativity* |
| **N** | **53** problems (Wikipedia count) |
| **Domain** | Mathematical general relativity (initial-value problem, mass, horizons, constraints) |
| **URL** | https://projecteuclid.org/download/pdf_1/euclid.pcma/1416335857 ; ANU CMA Proc. Vol. 19, pp. 244–268 |
| **Famous item #1** | Suitable quasi-local definition of energy–momentum → **Bartnik mass** research program |
| **Status** | **Mixed**: positive mass theorem already had Schoen–Yau / Witten proofs by then; quasi-local mass, constraint equations, and many geometric PDE problems remain active. Partial progress on many items; few “closed.” |

### 1.6 Barry Simon — Schrödinger / operator problems (1984 → 2000)

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| **Year** | 1984 list; refined **2000** list of 15 |
| **Author** | Barry Simon |
| **Titles** | *Fifteen Problems in Mathematical Physics* (1984); *Schrödinger Operators in the Twenty-First Century* (2000 Simon problems) |
| **N** | 15 (1984, broader); **15** (2000, Schrödinger-focused) |
| **Domain** | Spectral theory, Schrödinger operators, Anderson localization, Coulomb many-body |
| **URL** | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_problems ; MathWorld: https://mathworld.wolfram.com/SimonsProblems.html ; 1984 PDF often cited as Caltech R27 |
| **2000 themes** | Extended states (Anderson model); localization in 2D; absolute continuity / singular continuous spectrum; Coulomb energies; atomic ionization; etc. |
| **Status** | **Partially solved**. Artur Avila (Fields 2014) solved several including the **Ten Martini Problem**. Wikipedia: ≥3 of 2000 list solved; 1984 list had ~5 Schrödinger-related solved by 2000. Many localization / extended-state questions still open. |

### 1.7 Vitaly Ginzburg — Physics & astrophysics “physical minimum” (1971–2005)

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| **Year** | First list **1971** (17 items); major update **1999** (~30); reiterated through **2005** / Nobel materials |
| **Author** | Vitaly L. Ginzburg |
| **Title** | *What problems of physics and astrophysics seem now to be especially important and interesting…* |
| **N** | **30** (+ 3 “great problems”) |
| **Domain** | Broad physics + astrophysics (educational “physical minimum”) |
| **URL** | Usp. Fiz. Nauk / Phys. Usp. 1999: https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1070/PU1999v042n04ABEH000562 ; Nobel lecture PDF: https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/ginzburg-lecture.pdf ; Rutgers copy: https://www.physics.rutgers.edu/colloq/ginzburg.pdf |
| **The 30 (Ginzburg ~1999/2003)** | 1 Controlled fusion · 2 HTSC/RTSC · 3 Metallic hydrogen & exotic matter · 4 2D electron liquid / anomalous Hall · 5 Solid-state questions (heterostructures, mesoscopics…) · 6 Phase transitions / BEC / laser cooling · 7 Surface physics & clusters · 8 Liquid crystals / ferroelectrics · 9 Fullerenes & nanotubes · 10 Matter in superstrong B · 11 Nonlinear physics, turbulence, solitons, chaos · 12 X-ray / γ lasers · 13 Superheavy elements · 14 Mass spectrum, quarks, QCD, QGP · 15 Electroweak unification, W/Z, leptons · 16 SM, GUT, SUSY, proton decay, ν mass, monopoles · 17 Fundamental length / high-energy interactions · 18 CP nonconservation · 19 Nonperturbative vacuum & phase transitions · 20 Strings / M-theory · 21 Experimental GR tests · 22 Gravitational waves · 23 Cosmological constant / inflation / Λ · 24 Neutron stars, pulsars, supernovae · 25 Black holes, cosmic strings · 26 Quasars, galactic nuclei, galaxy formation · 27 Dark matter · 28 UHE cosmic rays · 29 Gamma-ray bursts · 30 Neutrino physics & astronomy |
| **Three “great problems”** | (i) Arrow of time / entropy increase; (ii) Interpretation of quantum mechanics; (iii) Origin of life from physics alone |
| **Status** | **Mixed (as intended)**. Detected: gravitational waves (LIGO 2015+), many neutrino oscillation facts, top quark, HTSC materials exist (mechanism open), fullerenes/nanotubes mainstream. Still open: RTSC mechanism/room-T ambient, dark matter identity, Λ problem, controlled ignition fusion as practical power, quark mass spectrum from first principles, etc. |

## 2. Millennium-era theoretical lists (≈2000)

### 2.1 Strings 2000 — “Physics Problems for the Next Millennium”

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| **Year** | 2000 (Strings 2000 conference) |
| **Selectors** | Michael Duff, David Gross, Edward Witten (from community submissions) |
| **N** | **10** |
| **Domain** | Fundamental / high-energy theory |
| **URL** | https://www.preskill.caltech.edu/millennium.html |
| **The 10** | 1 Dimensionless parameters calculable? (Gross) · 2 Quantum gravity & origin of universe (Witten) · 3 Proton lifetime (Gubser) · 4 Supersymmetry & breaking (Ferrara, Kane) · 5 Why 3+1 dimensions? · 6 Cosmological constant value / constancy · 7 M-theory degrees of freedom · 8 Black hole information paradox · 9 Hierarchy / Planck vs weak scale · 10 QCD confinement & mass gap |
| **Status (≈2025)** | **None conclusively solved.** Partial progress: LHC Higgs discovery without light superpartners (pressures SUSY-as-naturalness); AdS/CFT and holography advanced information-paradox discussion (Page curve, islands) without consensus resolution; cosmological constant still unexplained; confinement still Clay/Millennium-math open; proton lifetime bounds pushed higher, no observation. See Ethan Siegel 25-year update: https://bigthink.com/starts-with-a-bang/update-millennium-problems-physics/ |

## 4. Modern community roadmaps (facilities & funding)

### 4.1 Snowmass process (US particle physics)

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| **Years** | 1982 origin; major recent: **2013**, **2021** (report 2023) |
| **Org** | APS Division of Particles and Fields (+ sister divisions) |
| **Structure** | **10 Frontiers** (Snowmass 2021): Energy · Neutrino · Rare processes & precision · Cosmic · Theory · Accelerator · Instrumentation · Computation · Underground facilities · Community engagement |
| **URL** | https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.06581 · https://atlaswww.hep.anl.gov/snowmass21/ · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowmass_Process |
| **N** | Not a fixed problem count; multi-volume white papers → high-level report |
| **Domain** | High-energy physics vision + facilities |
| **Status** | Living planning process → feeds P5 prioritization. Questions (dark matter, neutrinos, Higgs naturalness, etc.) remain open; roadmap is about *how to attack them*. |
